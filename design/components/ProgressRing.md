Rings and bars show progress to a goal. Track is `line`, fill is `accent` or the domain color, caps are round, and the center shows the value in `stat` with a unit in `caption`. Sizes 64, 96, 120 with stroke 7, 10, 12. A completed goal switches the fill to `ok` and the center to `Done`.

**Do:** announce the value to screen readers (`128 of 160 grams of protein`).
**Don't:** use more than one ring color on a single ring.

Tokens: `line`, `accent`, `nutrition`, `ok`, `water`, `muted`.
