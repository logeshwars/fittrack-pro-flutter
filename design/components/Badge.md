Badges are 26px pills for status, rank, XP and macros. Status badges pair a soft fill with the matching text color (`ok-soft` + `ok`). Rank badges are outlined in their `tier-*` color with a dot, in three sizes (22, 26, 32).

**Do:** add an icon or word, never color alone; keep to one line.
**Don't:** use the raw domain color for text on Pearl; use `text-bad` for danger text.

Tokens: `ok-soft`, `warn-soft`, `bad-soft`, `accent-soft`, `tier-bronze` to `tier-legend`, `radius-pill`.
